Interactive Video Calibration Studio

Customize tripwires (entries) and staff / queue / dwell polygons per camera. Names are editable. Save hot-applies to the edge in ~3s. Cross-cam identity stays Future.

Tripwire — click A then B across the doorway/path. Set Entry = direction so walk-ins increment entries. Rename in the shapes list before Save.

Dwell / Queue — polygon ≥3 points, then Close Shape. Queue drives wait KPIs; dwell drives zone time.

Staff zone — behind counter / stock only. People who linger here are tagged staff and excluded from customer counts (sticky). Not facial recognition.

Multi-cam: use the header camera selector — each camera keeps its own tripwires & zones. Cross-cam ReID / unique store IDs = Future.
